Bella Napoli delivers authentic Neapolitan flavors right here in London – featuring classics like arancini, sfogliatelle, and world-famous Neapolitan pizza. Each bite is crafted to transport you straight to the historic pizzerias of Naples!

Who Are We?

We are Giulia & Marco, the founders of Bella Napoli London. Born and raised in Naples, we moved to London to share our family’s time-honored recipes and the warmth of Southern Italian hospitality.
